About Us

Durga Arts and Paintings has been creating wall murals and canvas paintings in Hyderabad for over 15 years. We have worked with Krishnaveni Schools, TVR Model Schools, Gowtham Model Schools and SR DG Schools, along with many play schools.

We create colourful, child-friendly themes that make learning fun. We also paint custom interior wall art for homes and offices, and canvas paintings.
